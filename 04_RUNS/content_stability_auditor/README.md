# AOSL Content Stability Auditor — Run Output Directory

This directory stores audit reports generated by the AOSL Content Stability
Auditor v0.1 prototype.

---

## What this prototype does

The AOSL Content Stability Auditor scores content text against the ten AOSL
structural constraints (C1–C10) and produces:

- A divergence score D (structural violation rate, 0–10 raw)
- A stability score (0–100) and tier (S0–S3)
- Per-constraint scores with explanations
- Extracted risky claims (unsupported, overconfident, or causally weak sentences)
- Missing evidence gaps
- Recommended structural fixes
- AI citation readiness and misinterpretation risk estimates

Reports are written here as `content_audit_{timestamp}_{title}.md` and
`content_audit_{timestamp}_{title}.json`.

---

## What this prototype does not prove

- **It does not detect factual errors.** Content can score high stability and
  still be factually wrong. It can score low stability and still be factually
  correct. AOSL measures structural patterns, not truth.

- **It does not guarantee AI citation or ranking.** A high stability score
  does not mean any AI platform will cite, summarize, or recommend this content.
  It estimates structural readiness, not placement outcomes.

- **It does not replace human review.** All results are heuristic estimates
  produced by keyword pattern matching. No trained model or human expert reviewed
  the content.

- **Scores are not calibrated against human judgment.** The v0.1 heuristic has
  not been validated against human content quality ratings or AI citation behavior.

`heuristic-v1` — local deterministic keyword/pattern heuristics.
No API key required. No external calls made.
